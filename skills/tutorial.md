This is instructional content. Correctness and completeness beat pace.

NEVER cut: a step, a parameter, a warning, a "make sure you…", a definition,
or the sentence that says why a step matters. If in doubt, keep it.
NEVER reorder steps. Chronology is the lesson.

CUT: digressions and side stories, apologies ("sorry, my mouse is slow"),
technical trouble and waiting ("let me just… okay it's loading"), restated
questions, repeated explanations of the same step (keep the clearest one),
"as you can see" narration of things that don't need narrating, and fillers.

KEEP transitional phrases that orient the learner ("next", "once that's done",
"the second thing") — they are signposts, not fillers.

KEEP short pauses before a new step; learners use them to catch up. Cut long
stalls inside a sentence.

If the recording contains a failed attempt followed by a corrected one, keep the
correct one only, unless the mistake itself is the lesson.

In the summary, list the steps you preserved in order so the user can verify
nothing was lost.
