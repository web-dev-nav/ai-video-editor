---
name: Clean-up
emoji: 🧹
order: 1
description: Light touch — remove fillers, stumbles, repeats and stalls; keep everything else exactly as spoken.
---
Goal: make the speaker sound like the best version of the same take. Nothing more.

- Remove only what a viewer would notice as a flaw: filler words and sounds, stutters,
  false starts, duplicated sentences (keep the best take), obvious stalls.
- Keep the structure, order and length of the talk. Do not shorten for its own sake.
- Keep natural breaths and short thinking pauses; they are part of the delivery.

WHAT COUNTS AS A FLAW — be thorough about these, they are the whole job:
- Filler sounds and words wherever they sit, including mid-sentence: um, uh, er, ah,
  mm, "like", "you know", "I mean", "sort of", "basically", "actually", "right?",
  "so" used as a throat-clear at the start of a sentence, and their equivalents in
  any other language (Hindi/Hinglish: मतलब, यार, वो, "haan to", …).
- Stutters and part-words: "the— the thing", "wh— what I mean".
- False starts: a sentence abandoned and begun again — keep only the finished one.
- Repeats: the same sentence or point delivered twice — keep the better take, cut
  the other completely, not just partly.
- Stalls: "let me think", "where was I", dead air inside a sentence.

HOW MUCH TO CUT: on a normal unrehearsed take this is typically 5–15% of the
runtime. Do not stop at the first few obvious "um"s — go through the whole
transcript and cut every instance. A pass that removes almost nothing is only
correct when the take genuinely is clean; if that is the case, say so plainly in
the summary rather than inventing cuts.

Never cut meaning: if removing a phrase changes what the speaker said, keep it.
