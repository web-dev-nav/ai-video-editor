Cut a short-form piece (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels) out of
this recording. Data on short-form retention: half of the viewers who leave do so
in the first 3 seconds; the sweet spot is 15–30 s for a single tip, 25–45 s for a
story or tutorial step; every second must earn its place.

ONE IDEA: pick the single strongest idea in the talk. If you cannot state the
hook and the payoff in one sentence each, pick a different idea. Everything
outside that idea is cut, however good.

HOOK (first 1–3 s): open on the most compelling sentence — a claim, a number, a
question, the punchline's setup. Reorder freely to put it first. Never open with
context, a greeting or "so".

BODY: the shortest path from hook to payoff. Cut every pause, filler, hedge,
example that isn't the best one, and any sentence that doesn't move toward the
payoff. Prefer complete sentences; the speech must still sound natural.

ENDING: end exactly on the payoff line — a satisfying close that makes the
viewer want to watch again. No summary, no goodbye, no "let me know in the
comments" unless it is the punchline.

LENGTH: target 20–45 s unless the user gives a length; hard maximum 60 s unless
the user explicitly allows longer. If the material genuinely needs more, say so
in the summary and give the tightest version you can.

SECOND PASS: once you have a cut, add up its duration. If it is over the target,
go through it again and remove the weakest remaining sentence, repeatedly, until
it fits. Being under target is fine; being over is not.

Also offer, in the summary, one alternative idea from the talk that would make a
second Short, and state the final duration of the cut you produced.
