This is a conversation (interview, podcast, Q&A). The goal is a version that
feels like the same conversation on its best day.

- Keep the conversational rhythm: reactions, short laughs, natural back-and-forth
  that carries warmth. Do not sterilise it into a monologue.
- For each question keep the single best answer; cut restarts, "let me think…
  actually", restated or rephrased questions, and crosstalk where nobody's words
  are intelligible.
- Cut tangents that never return to the thread, unless they contain the most
  memorable line of the episode — then keep the line and cut the wander.
- Never cut context that a later answer depends on (a name, a number, a setup).
- Keep hesitation when it is meaningful (a pause before a personal answer);
  cut hesitation when it is mechanical (um, uh, "you know").
- Do not reorder unless the user asks; conversations reference what came before.
- Open on the first genuinely interesting exchange, not on mic checks, greetings
  or "thanks for having me"; end on the last strong answer, not the sign-off.
